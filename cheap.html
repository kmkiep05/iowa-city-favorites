Cheap Things to Do in Iowa City This Weekend

Having fun in college does not have to mean spending your entire paycheck. From outdoor adventures to game nights and creative activities, here are fictional HawkSpot ideas for enjoying Iowa City while keeping more money in your wallet.

All listed discounts, prices, and offers are invented for this class project and are not available at actual businesses.

Free Activities

Free

Explore Downtown

Walk around downtown Iowa City, browse storefronts, discover interesting architecture, and take pictures with friends. Make a photo scavenger hunt by choosing five things to find along the way.

$0 per person

Best time: Saturday afternoon

Free

Host a Movie Night

Invite friends over, pick a theme, and let everyone vote on a movie. Bring blankets from home and ask each guest to contribute a snack they already have.

$0 if you use what you have

Best time: Friday evening

Free

Outdoor Reset

Take a walk, listen to a podcast, sketch outside, or bring a book to a peaceful outdoor spot. It is an easy way to recharge without buying a ticket or making a reservation.

$0 per person

Best time: Sunday morning

Free

Campus Photo Challenge

Team up with friends to capture ten photos inspired by Hawkeye colors, campus architecture, funny poses, and your favorite study spots. Compare everyone's pictures at the end.

$0 per person

Best time: Any afternoon

Create friendship bracelets, decorate tote bags, or make a collage. Share craft supplies with friends instead of purchasing a separate kit for everyone.

Under $10

Snack-and-Game Meetup

Bring a deck of cards or a board game to a friend's apartment. Everyone can contribute one inexpensive snack for a relaxed evening of games and conversation.

Estimated budget: $3–$7

Best for: A low-key Friday night

Example: A Weekend for $15

Want to make your budget stretch? Here is one fictional weekend plan that keeps the activity costs low.

  • Friday: Movie night using supplies at home — $0
  • Saturday: Downtown photo walk — $0
  • Saturday: Shared snack budget — $5
  • Sunday: Fictional café hot chocolate — $2
  • Sunday: DIY craft supplies — $8

Total fictional activity budget: $15 per person.

You can spend even less by skipping paid purchases, using supplies you already own, and choosing free public activities.

Smart Ways to Save

Plan Ahead

Decide how much you want to spend before the weekend starts. Choose one paid activity and fill the rest of your schedule with free options.

Share Costs

For appropriate group activities, split the cost of shared supplies or snacks. Agree on a budget first so everyone feels comfortable.

Look for Student Offers

Check official business websites and ask venues whether they offer student pricing. Bring your student ID when a real discount requires verification.

Prices and promotions on this page are fictional examples. Check directly with real businesses for current offers, eligibility rules, and event details before spending money.
