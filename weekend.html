Your Weekend Guide

Things to Do in the Iowa City Area This Weekend

Whether you want to get outside, discover downtown, catch a game-day atmosphere, or spend time with friends, there's always a new way to plan your weekend. Start with these fictional HawkSpot event ideas.

The activities, schedules, prices, and venues on this page are invented for a class project, not real local listings.

Friday: Kick Off the Weekend

5:00–7:00 p.m.

Downtown Art & Photo Walk

Meet fictional student host Maya Brooks at the made-up Ped Mall Welcome Point. Explore colorful storefronts, look for interesting public art, and take photos with your friends.

Location: Downtown Iowa City

Fictional cost: Free

Good for: Friends and photography fans

7:30–9:30 p.m.

Campus Corner Game Night

Spend the evening playing trivia, cards, and board games at the fictional Campus Corner Lounge. Teams can compete for a made-up HawkSpot champion title.

Location: Fictional Campus Corner Lounge

Fictional cost: Free entry

Good for: Meeting people and friendly competition

Saturday: Get Out and Explore

9:00–11:00 a.m.

Hawkeye Morning Walk

Start your morning with a relaxed walk along a local trail. Bring a water bottle, wear comfortable shoes, and invite a friend who wants a break from studying.

Location: Iowa City area trails

Fictional cost: Free

Good for: Fresh air and light exercise

12:00–2:00 p.m.

Student Street-Food Social

Imagine a courtyard filled with food stalls, music, and picnic tables. This fictional pop-up invites students to sample different flavors and vote for their favorite lunch.

Location: Fictional Riverfront Courtyard

Fictional cost: $5–$12 per person

Good for: Food lovers and groups

Afternoon

Hawkeye Sports Watch Party

Put on black and gold, meet friends, and enjoy the excitement of college sports at a fictional student watch party. Check official University of Iowa athletics information for real game schedules.

Location: Fictional Black & Gold Social Club

Fictional cost: Free entry

Good for: Sports fans

Sunday: Recharge Before Monday

10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.

Cozy Café & Journaling

Bring a journal, sketchbook, or book and settle in for a quiet morning at the fictional Red Brick Café. Reflect on the past week and make a simple plan for the next one.

Location: Fictional Red Brick Café

Fictional budget: $3–$8 for a drink or snack

1:00–3:00 p.m.

Creative Craft Meetup

Make friendship bracelets, decorate a notebook, or create a collage with other students. Bring supplies you already have and trade materials to keep the afternoon inexpensive.

Location: Fictional HawkSpot Student Hub

Fictional cost: $2 suggested supply contribution

Build Your Own Weekend

Pair a free morning walk with a downtown afternoon, then finish with game night. For a lower-cost weekend, bring snacks from home, share transportation when appropriate, and choose activities that do not require tickets.

Remember that all events and prices above are fictional examples. Confirm real venue details, opening hours, and official sports schedules before making actual plans.
